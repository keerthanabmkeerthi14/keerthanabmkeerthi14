# Code of Conduct

This is my GitHub profile repository. It shares my work, skills and ways to collaborate. I want it to be a welcoming and professional space for everyone who visits.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful, kind and direct.
- Focus on ideas, code and trade-offs, not on people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect privacy: never share personal information or confidential work.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, unrelated promotion or repeated unsolicited messages.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Posting secrets, tokens or security-sensitive details in public.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any collaboration that starts from this repository.

## Enforcement

I may edit, hide, lock or delete content that breaks these rules. Serious or repeated violations may lead to blocking or reporting through GitHub. To raise a concern, contact me through the channels in the README.
